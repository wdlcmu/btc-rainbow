How does bitcoin perform after a halving?

Bitcoin Rainbow is a free site in plain words. It shows where bitcoin's price sits in its own past.

The short answer: the price rose in the year after each halving. The gain shrank each time. And four is a tiny sample.

See it: the first two years of each cycle

Each line starts at 100 on its halving day. The height of the line is the price, as a multiple of that day's price.

  • 2012 — up 7,620% in year one; ×30 at two years
  • 2016 — up 285% in year one; ×10 at two years
  • 2020 — up 538% in year one; ×3.5 at two years
  • 2024, this cycle — up 33% in year one; ×1.2 at two years

Log scale: each marked step up the side is 10 times the one below it. Drawn from weekly closes in this site's own daily record. Four cycles is four — too few to call a law. The lines show the past, not a promise.

Read the pattern with care

Four halvings is four. That is too few to call a law.

The one-year gain went 7,620%, then 285%, then 538%, then 33%.

And each cycle high was followed by a long, deep fall.

The pattern is real in the record. The record is short. The past is not a promise.
