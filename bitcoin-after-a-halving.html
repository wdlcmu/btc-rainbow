How does bitcoin perform after a halving?

Bitcoin Rainbow is a free site in plain words. It shows where bitcoin's price sits in its own past.

The short answer: the price rose in the year after each halving. The gain shrank each time. And four is a tiny sample.

Read the pattern with care

Four halvings is four. That is too few to call a law.

The one-year gain went 7,620%, then 285%, then 538%, then 33%.

And each cycle high was followed by a long, deep fall.

The pattern is real in the record. The record is short. The past is not a promise.
