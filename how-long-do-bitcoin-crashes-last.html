How long do bitcoin crashes last?

The big falls took about a year to reach the low. The climb back to the old top took about 2 to 3 years in all.

Here is every big fall, counted in days from the daily price record.

The big falls, in days

FallDepthDays downTop to top again
2011Down 93%164 days630 days
2013 to 2015Down 85%406 days1,177 days
2017 to 2018Down 83%364 days1,080 days
Spring 2021Down 53%98 days189 days
2021 to 2022Down 77%378 days847 days
2025 to nowDown 53%267 days to the low so farNot back yet

Top to top again means: the first day the price closed above the old top.

Small falls are much shorter

Falls of a fifth to a third happened ten more times in this record. Most ended in weeks. The rest took a few months.

Depth is the thing to watch. The deeper the fall, the longer the wait has been.

What this page is not saying

Past falls all ended. That is a fact of the past.

This table is not a clock for the next fall. One day, a fall may not come back.
