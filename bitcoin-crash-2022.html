The bitcoin crash of 2022

The fall on the rainbow. Pink shading: the fall itself, Nov 2021 to Nov 2022.

Bitcoin topped at $67,562 in Nov 2021, then fell for just over a year to $15,760 in Nov 2022 — down 77%.

About one dollar in four was left at the low.

The fall, step by step

Nov 22, 2022$15,760Down 77%. The low.

The last drop came in Nov 2022, when a big exchange, FTX, failed and lots of people sold at once.

The climb back

The price first closed above the old top on Mar 5, 2024, at $68,378 — 847 days after the top, about 2 years and 4 months.

What this page is not saying

This crash ended. That is a fact of the past. It does not mean the next crash ends — one day, a fall may not come back.
