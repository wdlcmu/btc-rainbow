Other rainbow charts are built for traders, with buy and sell labels. This one is in plain words, made to tap and explore. It also tells you what the chart cannot do.

What it cannot do

The line is fit to the past. It cannot see what comes next. Blockchain Center made this chart famous, and its keeper has said in writing that it was never a serious model. We keep that warning on the chart, not under it.

Where does the price sit in its long story?

What the colors mean.

The line is bitcoin’s long-run trend price: about … today. It is where the price would sit if bitcoin had grown at its long-run pace, fit to every daily price since 2010. Above the line means ahead of its own history. Below it means behind it. Nine colored bands stack around the line. Low bands: far under its own past growth. High bands: far over it.

Nine colors, grouped five ways, low to high. Prices are today’s. Tap a color to read its story.

Trivia

Guess first: seven questions from the record.

Each answer is counted from the same daily record the chart is drawn from, through Nov 2, 2026. Tap a guess. The record answers back.

1. Our record starts on Aug 18, 2010. What did one bitcoin cost that day?

Seven cents. The whole climb on the chart starts there.

2. How many bitcoins will ever exist?

21 million, ever. The cap is set in the code. New coins still arrive, but the pace halves about each four years.

3. What is the worst fall in the record, from a top to the low that came after it?

In 2011 the price fell 93%: from about $34 to about $2. Hackers had broken into Mt. Gox, the one big exchange, and faith in the young market drained away. The climb back to the top took 630 days.

4. About each four years, the pay for making new blocks is cut in half. When was the last cut?

Apr 19, 2024. The pay fell to 3.125 new coins per block. The next cut lands about 2028.

5. The price has come into the Deep Blue band 92 times. When a full year had passed after a visit began, how often did that year end higher?

Every time so far: 84 of 84 visits. Those years ended up 29% to 4,715%. But the line was drawn with hindsight, through prices that were already known. That is the past. It is not a promise.

6. What is the highest daily close in the whole record?

$124,777, on Oct 7, 2025. The last close in the record, Nov 2, 2026, sat 37% under that top.

7. Of every 100 days since 2010, about how many has the price spent in the Deep Blue band?

About 23 of every 100 days: 1,379 days in all. No band holds more days. Deep Blue is less a rare alarm than a place this price knows well.
