Step 1

What bitcoin is.

Bitcoin is money for the internet. No company runs it. No boss can print more. Only 21 million will ever exist. It lives on many computers at once. You can own a whole coin, or a small piece.

Step 2

Where are we now?

It is a growth chart for bitcoin's price. Through the middle runs one line: bitcoin's long-run growth, fit to every daily price on record. The chart is drawn in percents, not dollars. A jump from $1 to $2 takes the same space as a jump from $50,000 to $100,000, so the whole history fits on one page. Around the line stack nine colored bands, Deep Blue at the bottom, Red at the top. What the colors mean.

Tap a color on the chart, or a band in the list. Each band tells you three things: how far from the line it sits, how many days the price has lived there, and what the year after past visits did.

So, does this mean buy?

No. The chart shows where the price sits inside its own past pattern. It cannot say buy, and it cannot say skip. No chart can.

What it can do is show what happened the other times the price sat where it sits now. That is the band panel above, and the day scrubber under the chart.

How to read this chart.

The bands are a ruler, not a road. Bitcoin has sat in every band. It fell hard from high bands. It fell hard from mid bands too.

A model, not prophecy. This line shows the past. It does not know the future. No one does. Past prices never promise future ones.

Step 3

Why we think it climbs on. A belief, not a fact.

Its line climbs for 16 years. It fell hard, many times. It climbed back each time. Pick any start day. Four years on, the price stood higher. Every time, so far.

Why might it go on? Only 21 million will ever exist. Most are already out. New coins come slower every four years. The last cut was in 2024. Big funds sell it in stock accounts. That is the case: a belief, not a fact. This is learning, not financial advice.

Step 5

It swings hard. We could be wrong.

Since 2011, it fell by half or more. Seven times. In 2022, it fell by about three fourths. It can do that again. A total loss should sting, not sink you.

It could fall and stay down. New laws could hurt it. The climb is a pattern, not a law. Many fans keep 5 to 10 in 100 saved. The bold end is 10 to 20 in 100. Only money you will not need for years. This is learning, not financial advice.
