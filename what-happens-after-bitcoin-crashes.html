What happens after bitcoin crashes?

So far, each big fall has ended. The price climbed back to its old top. It took years, not weeks.

The newest fall is still open.

The four biggest falls

WhenThe fallBack to the top
2011$34 to $2, down 93%About 2 years
2013 to 2015$1,137 to $172, down 85%About 3 years
2017 to 2018$19,280 to $3,232, down 83%About 3 years
2021 to 2022$67,562 to $15,760, down 77%About 2 years

Falls of half or more are a normal part here. They are the price of the climbs.

It does not come back in a straight line

In spring 2021, the price fell by half. It bounced, then fell much more that winter.

The first low was $29,790, in Jul 2021. One year on, the price was $23,227. Lower, not higher.

A bounce is not a promise either.

The fall that has not ended

The top was Oct 2025, at $124,777. By Jul 2026, the price was $58,534. That is a fall of 53%.

At the end of Oct 2026, it sat near $81,000. Still well below the old top.

This fall has not come back yet.

What this page is not saying

Past falls all ended. That is a fact of the past.

No fall is owed an ending. One day, a fall may not come back. No chart can rule that out.
