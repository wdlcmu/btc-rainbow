What happens after bitcoin crashes?

So far, each big fall has ended, and the price climbed back to its old top — in years, not weeks. The newest fall is still open.

Pink: the biggest falls. Gold dots: the day the price passed its old top again. The newest fall has no dot.

Falls of half or more are normal here. They are the price of the climbs.

It does not come back in a straight line

In spring 2021, the price fell by half. It bounced, then fell much more that winter: the first low was $29,790, in Jul 2021, and one year on the price was $23,227 — lower, not higher.

A bounce is not a promise either.

The fall that has not ended

The top was Oct 2025, at $124,777. By Jul 2026, the price was $58,534 — a fall of 53%. At the end of Oct 2026, it sat near $81,000, still well below the old top. This fall has not come back yet.

What this page is not saying

Past falls all ended. That is a fact of the past.

No fall is owed an ending. One day, a fall may not come back. No chart can rule that out.
