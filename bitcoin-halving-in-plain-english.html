What is a bitcoin halving?

New coins are made with each new block, as the block's pay. Each 210,000 blocks, that pay is cut in half. That cut is the halving.

Think of a well that gives less every few years: the flow halves each time, and in time slows to near zero.

The pay per block, drawn to scale. Each cut halves it.

210,000 blocks take about four years. So cuts land about each four years.

What comes next

The next cut falls at block 1,050,000 — at the past pace of blocks, about 2028. The date is set by block count, not by a calendar, so treat 2028 as a rough guide, not a fixed date.

With all cuts in place, only 21 million coins will ever be made.

This page says what the halving is — not what the price will do. No one can know that from this fact alone.
