Has bitcoin ever lost money over four years?

No. Not once, so far.

The count

Pick any start day since Aug 2010. Move four years on, same coin. The price stood higher at the end.

That is 4,460 four-year stretches. All 4,460 ended higher.

Over ten years, the story is the same. All 2,270 ten-year stretches ended higher.

The worst stretch still ended up

The worst start day was Apr 9, 2021, at $58,049. Four years on, the price was $76,267 — even the worst stretch ended about a third higher.

The middle can still hurt

An end can hide a hard middle: buy at the Dec 2017 top, $19,280, and one year on the price was $3,232 — a fall of 83%.

Four years on, it stood at $47,658. The end was fine; the middle was a crash.

Holding through the middle is the hard part.

Why two words do the work

"So far" is part of the answer. Sixteen years is a short life for money, and streaks in markets end with no warning. The record is the past. It is not a promise.
