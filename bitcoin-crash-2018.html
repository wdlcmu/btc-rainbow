The bitcoin crash of 2018

Bitcoin topped at $19,280 in Dec 2017. Then it fell for a year. The low was $3,232 in Dec 2018, down 83%.

Of every six dollars of worth at the top, about one dollar was left.

The fall, step by step

DayPriceFall from the top
Dec 17, 2017$19,280The top

Dec 16, 2018$3,232Down 83%. The low.

The first drop was fast. Two thirds of the price was gone in seven weeks.

Then came the slow part: ten more months of grind, down to the low.

The climb back

The low was not the end of the wait. The price first closed above the old top on Dec 1, 2020, at $19,710.

That is 1,080 days after the top. About three years, top to top.

What this page is not saying

This crash ended. That is a fact of the past.

It does not mean the next crash ends. No fall is owed an ending.
