The bitcoin crash of 2018

The fall on the rainbow. Pink shading: the fall itself, Dec 2017 to Dec 2018.

Bitcoin topped at $19,280 in Dec 2017, then fell for a year to $3,232 in Dec 2018 — down 83%.

Of every six dollars of worth at the top, about one dollar was left.

The fall, step by step

Dec 16, 2018$3,232Down 83%. The low.

The first drop took two thirds of the price in seven weeks. Then ten slow months of grind to the low.

The climb back

The low was not the end of the wait. The price first closed above the old top on Dec 1, 2020, at $19,710 — 1,080 days after the top, about three years.

What this page is not saying

This crash ended. That is a fact of the past. It does not mean the next crash ends — no fall is owed an ending.
