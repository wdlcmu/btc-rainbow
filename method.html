How do we know any of this?

Sources & method.

The model

Rainbow model, our own fit, version 1. One line fit to every daily close on record:

ln(price) = −37.28 + 5.60 × ln(days since Jan 3, 2009)

Fit window: Aug 18, 2010 to Nov 2, 2026. 5,921 daily closes in all. Fit score (R²): 0.96, in log terms. That score grades the fit to the past. It is not a promise about the future.

Exact fit, full precision: a = −37.27685094738432, b = 5.5958560618226985.

Nine bands stack on the line. Each is the same step wide: about 1.35 times the price below it. The ladder runs from 0.47 to 7.03 times the line.

Band edges, as multiples of the line: 0.47–0.64, 0.64–0.86, 0.86–1.16, 1.16–1.57, 1.57–2.12, 2.12–2.86, 2.86–3.86, 3.86–5.21, 5.21–7.03.

First drawn by azop in 2014, on trolololo's growth line. Blockchain Center made it famous. This fit is our own, on the same public prices. A fit describes the past. It is not a forecast.

Where each part comes from

The chart, the gauges, and the falls. Daily closes from blockchain.info through Oct 29, 2026, then Coinbase daily candles: blockchain.info's feed stopped, and Coinbase's start-of-day price samples the same moment blockchain.info's daily value does. The live price is from Coinbase.

The network. Block height, hashrate, and fees from mempool.space. Supply is checked against blockchain.info.

Fear & Greed. The Fear & Greed Index by Alternative.me, shown with its maker's name next to the number.

Bitcoin's share. CoinGecko's count of the whole crypto market.

Computed from the snapshot shipped with this page: daily closes through Nov 2, 2026.

What to watch for

Prices here are daily closes. A swing inside one day can be bigger than the day shows. In June 2011, one exchange flashed to one cent for a few minutes. The daily closes say that fall was 93%. Both are true, of different things.

The fit score (R²) grades how well the line hugs the past. It says nothing about next year.

Bitcoin's share of all crypto depends on who counts the coins. Sites disagree by a point or two. Ours comes from CoinGecko, named on the card.

The Fear & Greed score is one outside index, made by Alternative.me. We show it with its maker's name next to it, and we do not fold it into any other number here.

The model explorer on the What-if page stretches the model's own lines forward. Those lines are labelled as the model's, never as a forecast.

The standing line

This is analysis and education, not financial advice. Numbers here are computed from real data and stamped with the day they describe. When a source is quiet, its card says so instead of showing an old number as new.

So, does this mean buy?

No. The chart shows where the price sits inside its own past pattern. It cannot say buy, and it cannot say skip. No chart can.

What it can do: show what happened the other times the price sat where it sits now. See the band list on the front page, or scrub its chart.
