Small buys, each week. What would have happened?

DCA means you buy a set sum on a set day, week by week, no matter the price.

Think of an allowance, in reverse. A little bit goes in each week.

How this tool works

Pick $10, $25, $50, or $100 a week, and 1, 3, or 5 years. The tool buys once a week at real past prices, adds up your coins, and values them at the live price.

Most such tools use months, not weeks. Some show a made-up future. This one shows the past only.
