What is Bitcoin? In plain words.

Bitcoin is cash for the web. You can send it to a friend. No bank sits in the middle.

A shared list

Think of a list in a town square: all can read it, and no one owns it. When you pay a friend, the list gets a new line saying you sent the coins.

The list is kept on lots of PCs at once, all holding the same list. That is why no one firm can change it.

A hard cap

Only 21 million coins will ever be made. That cap is set in the code. New coins come out at a set pace. The pace drops by half each four years or so.

The first block was made on Jan 3, 2009. Our chart counts days from that date.

Price is just a tag

Price is the tag at the store. It changes each day. On Oct 31, 2026, the tag said $77,471 for one coin. A year before, it said $109,553.

You can own a small slice of a coin — no need to buy a whole one.
