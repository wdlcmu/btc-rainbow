What today's reading means

The chart stacks nine colored bands around one line. The line is bitcoin's long-run growth. It is fit to every daily price since 2010.

Low bands mean the price sits far under its own past growth. High bands mean far over it.

The bands take no side. They say where the price sits. They do not say what comes next.
