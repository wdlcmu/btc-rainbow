What is the bitcoin rainbow chart?

Bitcoin Rainbow is a free site in plain words. It shows where bitcoin's price sits in its own past.

The rainbow chart is a growth chart for bitcoin's price. It answers one question: where does today's price sit in the whole story?

The chart, in one live example

Reading the chart…

One line, nine bands

Through the middle runs one line. It is fit to every daily price since 2010.

The line is bitcoin's long-run growth, in one stroke.

Around the line stack nine colored bands. Deep Blue sits at the bottom. Red sits at the top.

Low bands: the price sits far under its own past growth. High bands: far over it.

Here, band names are colors only. No band says buy. No band says sell.

Where the chart came from

A Reddit user, azop, drew the first rainbow in 2014.

He drew it on a growth line by trolololo, a forum user.

Blockchain Center made the chart famous. This site's line is its own fit, on the same public prices.

What the chart cannot do

The line is fit to the past. It cannot see what comes next.

The chart's famous keeper has said so in writing. It was never a serious model.

Read it as a map of the past. Not as a promise.
