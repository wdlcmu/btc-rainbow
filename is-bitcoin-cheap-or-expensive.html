How the count works

One line is fit to every daily price since 2010. The line is bitcoin's long-run growth.

Each past day gets one mark: how far its price sat from the line that day.

Then we count. On how many days did the price sit at or below today's mark?

What the count cannot say

Low against its own past is not the same as cheap. A thing can sit low and fall more.

The line is fit to the past, with hindsight. It can be wrong about what comes next.

This page counts. It does not call. The buy call is yours, made calmly.
